2020.06.01

Our arrangements after the state of emergency was lifted on 25 May

Our sincere sympathies go to everyone who has contracted COVID-19, and to their families and those around them.
Following the lifting of the state of emergency on 25 May, we have decided to operate as set out below for the time being.

Period: From Monday 1 June 2020, until further notice
Daikanyama office: We work from home as a rule, and carry out the following at the office.
· Dealing with new enquiries.
· Occasional renovation seminars
· General correspondence and work on live projects.
(Other than client meetings: weekdays 10:00 to 18:00, excluding weekends and public holidays)
Staff: Working from home
Sites under construction: Work proceeds after everyone's temperature is checked that morning
Hand washing
Masks worn
Hand sanitiser provided
The instructions of each building management association are followed
Clients whose homes have been handed over: Please contact us by the means already given to you.
New enquiries: info@timelessliving.jp — please get in touch and we will take it from there.
