A fine space built in lime plaster: an apartment renovation seminar for the later years will be held.

Timeless Living works with materials suited to Japan's climate — lime plaster among them — to make, through renovation, city apartments that grow more comfortable and more beautiful as the years pass.
In this seminar we explain what makes an apartment renovation in lime plaster worth doing, drawing on projects we have completed, and we do so carefully, with one party at a time.
Whether you are already considering renovating an apartment or are only beginning to think about it, you are very welcome to join us.

Completed projects

TIMELESS LIVING Mejiro

To answer a wish to live in a house of lime plaster, this couple began with the search for the apartment itself, renovation already in mind. A full skeleton renovation let the plan be reconsidered substantially, and the layout built from scratch around the way they live. Walls and ceilings in every room are finished in lime plaster; the floors are natural materials, wool-linen carpet and solid timber. A home for unhurried hours: comfortable air, the cherry blossom in view, and the Scandinavian furniture they chose.

TIMELESS LIVING Shimazuyama

A fine apartment renovation where real things are shown at their best — Scandinavian vintage, furniture by Poul Kjærholm. Walls and ceilings finished in lime plaster, and a terrace deck in the same solid teak as the living room, join the home to its private garden and make an open, settled space. Niches are set into the limestone wall facing the entrance and into the living-room fireplace, so that something can be put out for each season. A home appointed with the ease of a detached house.
