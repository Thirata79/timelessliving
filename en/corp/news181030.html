Kisaburo Morioka: Beyond colour

Continuing the theme of art in everyday life that we have pursued since 2012, we are holding an exhibition by the photographer Kisaburo Morioka — his first with us in the three years since 2015.
What to look for this time is the limitless space and time that open out from the colour within a landscape.

Kisaburo Morioka

1951 Born in Okayama
1975–1977 Studied in Sweden
1984 First solo exhibition
1989 Moved to the United States
2012 Returned to Japan
Has exhibited in Japan, the United States and Sweden since his first solo show.

Exhibition details

Dates Saturday 10 to Saturday 17 November 2018, 11:00–19:00
Opening reception Saturday 10 November 201818: 00～21: 00
Place Timeless Living concept room
Address Courthouse Aobadai 1F, 1-2-6 Aobadai, Meguro, Tokyo
8 minutes' walk from Daikanyama station on the Tokyu Toyoko line (map here)
