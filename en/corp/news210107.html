2021.01.07

Our response to the state of emergency declared on 7 January

In light of the spread of COVID-19, we have decided to operate as set out below for the time being.
We are sorry for the inconvenience, and ask for your understanding and cooperation.　　

Period: From Friday 8 January 2021, until further notice
Daikanyama office: Closed
Calls are diverted during the hours given below (weekdays 10:00 to 18:00, excluding weekends and public holidays).
Staff: Working from home
Sites under construction: Work proceeds after everyone's temperature is checked that morning
Hand washing
Masks worn
Hand sanitiser provided
The instructions of each building management association are followed
Clients whose homes have been handed over: Please contact us by the means already given to you.
New enquiries: info@timelessliving.jp — please get in touch and we will take it from there.
