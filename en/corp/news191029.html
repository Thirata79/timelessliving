2019.10.29

Yoko Morioka: PENUMBRA

Continuing the theme of art in everyday life that we have pursued since 2012, we are holding an exhibition by the photographer Yoko Morioka.

This is the first exhibition in the three years since November 2016.
This time her subject is the beauty of light woven by a shadow and its surroundings. Each work gathers, carefully, the beauty of a moment we pass over without noticing, and each is freshly moving.

Yoko Morioka

1986 First solo exhibition
1989 Moved to the United States; has lived in New York since
2012 Returned to Japan
Solo and group exhibitions in New York, New Jersey, Stockholm, Tokyo, Yokohama and Okayama

Exhibition details

Dates Saturday 9 to Saturday 16 November 2019, 11:00–19:00
Opening reception Saturday 9 November 201918:00～21:00
Place Timeless Living concept room
Address Courthouse Aobadai 1F, 1-2-6 Aobadai, Meguro, Tokyo
8 minutes' walk from Daikanyama station on the Tokyu Toyoko line (map here)
