Special apartment renovation seminar
“How a timeless home is made” will be held.

Timeless Living works with materials suited to Japan's climate — lime plaster and solid timber among them — to make, through renovation, city apartments that grow more comfortable and more beautiful as the years pass.

In this seminar we explain how such a timeless home is made, drawing on projects we have completed, and we do so carefully, with one party at a time.
If you are about to start looking for an older apartment, we will also explain what to look for in a property that suits renovation. Please do come along.

The materials a timeless home is made of

Lime plaster walls and ceilings

For walls and ceilings we propose lime plaster. The plaster we work with is 100% natural. Lime plaster has been used in Japan for centuries, and it is excellent for fire resistance, for regulating humidity, and for safety.
Laid by a skilled plasterer, smooth and yet full of character, a lime plaster wall does what vinyl wallcovering cannot: it takes the shadows of the lighting beautifully.

Solid timber flooring

Flooring ranges from the common printed wood-grain plywood — wood waste bound with synthetic adhesive — to boards cut from a single piece of solid timber. The first has a glossy surface, and some are coated to resist scratching. They are easy to look after, but they do not last.
