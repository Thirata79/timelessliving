TIMELESS LIVING Ariake

A family of three, with a small child, looking for an apartment with renovation already in mind. What they chose was a flat in a tower block on the bay. The positions and the design of the doors were reworked so that the living room reads as open, and the bedroom, wrapped in lime plaster, became a gentle room in which one can rest. The hallway was deliberately widened to make an entrance space like a small gallery, which also solved the problem of the kitchen being visible from the corridor. The kitchen was separated from the living and dining room, and walling it gave surfaces on which furnishings and art are easy to show. A home that never feels small for all its compactness: wrapped in natural materials and lime plaster, light, open and easy to be in.

Specification

Lime plaster walls and ceilings
A 100% natural lime plaster containing no adhesive, excellent for fire resistance, humidity regulation and safety.
Laid by a skilled plasterer, smooth and full of character. Because it regulates humidity it resists condensation, and it absorbs household smells, which makes for a comfortable room.
Thick-veneer solid ash flooring
A 3 mm thick-veneer solid ash floor. Ash is a hard timber, used for furniture and for baseball bats, and it reads light and brisk. Finished in natural oil; the sheen grows with time.
Kitchen made to order
A single-run kitchen made to order. The back run is the same depth as the kitchen itself, which gives ample storage.
Wooden blinds
Wooden blinds at every window, framed in white for a clean, uncluttered look. They control the strong sunlight particular to a tower block, while the soft reflected light, together with the white of the plaster, fills the whole room.
