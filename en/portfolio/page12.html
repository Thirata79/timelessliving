TIMELESS LIVING Hiroo 3

A renovation at Hiroo Garden Hills, a vintage block that suits antique furniture, bringing the trees in front of it into the living and dining room. A walnut wall running the length of one side draws you from the entrance towards a bright living room in which the green of the trees shows well; the air made by natural materials — lime plaster, wool-linen carpet — and the antiques bought while the family lived in England sit together easily, and time runs slowly. In the bedroom the walls and ceiling are finished in a pale Farrow & Ball water-based paint and the floor is laid in wool carpet, which softens the room. The kitchen, left semi-open with a large serving counter so that the family can talk while looking at the greenery in the window, is walled around so that it does not assert itself in the living room.

Specification

Lime plaster walls and ceilings
A 100% natural lime plaster containing no adhesive, excellent for fire resistance, humidity regulation and safety.
Laid by a skilled plasterer, smooth and full of character. Because it regulates humidity it resists condensation, and it absorbs household smells, which makes for a comfortable room.
Carpet in natural fibres
The living and dining floor is a Belgian carpet combining the durability of linen with the softness of wool: hard-wearing enough for shoes, yet pleasant under a bare foot. It is laid without the adhesive normally used.
Kitchen made to order
A semi-open kitchen in pale grey, made to order. A wall oven and a large serving counter keep the design uncluttered. The counter gives the family a place to talk while looking at the greenery in the window.
Walls in English paint
The bedroom walls and ceiling are finished in an English water-based paint. Unlike vinyl wallcovering it takes lighting softly. It is free of the volatile organic compounds (VOCs) harmful to people, and at home it has been used in the restoration of castles. Natural pigments, pale colours, no smell, a smooth finish.
