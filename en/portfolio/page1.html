TIMELESS LIVING Shimazuyama

A fine apartment renovation where real things are shown at their best — Scandinavian vintage, furniture by Poul Kjærholm. It occupies the ground floor of a low-rise vintage block on the high ground of Shimazuyama, in the quiet residential Jonan Gozan district. Walls and ceilings finished in lime plaster, and a terrace deck in the same solid teak as the living room, join the home to its private garden and make an open, settled space. Niches are set into the limestone wall facing the entrance and into the living-room fireplace, so that something can be put out for each season. With a jacuzzi bath among other things, this is a home appointed with the ease of a detached house.

Specification

Lime plaster walls and ceilings
A 100% natural lime plaster containing no adhesive, excellent for fire resistance, humidity regulation and safety. Laid by a skilled plasterer, smooth and full of character.
Solid teak flooring
Solid teak 15 mm thick, suitable for use over underfloor heating.
Finished in natural oil; with time the oils in the timber itself deepen the colour and the sheen. Each board is screw-fixed by hand, so the floor is laid with no adhesive.
Rather than fixing straight to the concrete slab, we build a timber floor beneath it, which gives the floor a little spring and makes it good to walk on.
Solid teak deck terrace
The same solid teak as the living room. Teak has been used for ships' decks for centuries, and with regular maintenance it can be used outdoors. Set level with the living-room floor, it is designed to read as an extension of the room.
