Privacy policy

Timeless Living Co., Ltd. (hereafter “the Company”) recognises the importance of protecting personal information. The Company has established the following policy on the acquisition, use and management of personal information provided by customers, has made it known throughout its officers and staff, and observes it without exception.

1. The purpose of this policy
To protect personal information and to safeguard the rights and interests of our customers.
2. Handling of personal information
In handling personal information in connection with its property business, the Company has established a management structure for the protection of personal information appropriate to how it actually operates, and handles such information properly and carefully in accordance with internal rules.
(1) Acquisition of personal information
When acquiring personal information the Company makes the purpose of use clear to the customer, and uses lawful and fair means to the extent necessary to achieve that purpose. The purposes for which the Company uses personal information are as follows: the sales activities of the Company's business (visits, direct mail, approaches by telephone, approaches by email and the like); analysis of customer trends and research and analysis for product planning; and aftercare.
(2) Use of personal information
The Company uses personal information only within the scope of the purposes published in advance.
(3) Provision of personal information to third parties
The Company does not disclose to third parties the personal information customers provide or register, except in any of the following cases.
[1] Where the customer has consented.
[2] Where it is necessary to disclose personal information to a contractor with which the Company has an agreement on the protection of personal information, in order to carry out the purpose of collection stated to the customer.
[3] Where the information has been processed into statistical data in which an individual customer cannot be identified.
[4] Where a request carrying a legal obligation is received from a public authority.
[5] Where required by law.
(4) Supervision of contractors
Where the handling of personal information is entrusted in whole or in part to a contractor, the Company concludes a contract including confidentiality provisions and exercises the necessary and appropriate supervision to ensure that the contractor manages personal information securely.
3. Security measures
To ensure the accuracy and security of personal information, the Company implements security measures, information security measures among them, and reliably prevents unauthorised access to personal information and its loss, destruction, alteration or leakage. Where improvement is found to be necessary as a result of internal audit, from instances of security incidents in the market, or from customer requests, the Company corrects the matter promptly.
4. Compliance with laws and standards
To ensure the protection of personal information, the Company complies with the laws relating to personal information and with the guidelines of the bodies to which it belongs.
5. Respect for customers' rights
The Company respects the rights of its customers and will respond without fail where a customer requests disclosure, correction or deletion of their own information, or objects to its use or provision.
6. Security and links
This privacy policy applies to use of this website. The Company takes the greatest care over the handling of the personal information of customers who use this website, so that it is properly protected.
(1) To secure personal information while it is being sent and received, the Company uses SSL encrypted communication. Encrypting the information prevents its leakage or alteration. However, by the nature of the internet, security in transit cannot be guaranteed to be complete.
(2) The Company accepts no responsibility for the protection of customers' personal information on other websites linked from its own web pages.
