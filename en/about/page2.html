Our design

How we think about design

Design you feel at ease in

Even in a city apartment, the hours spent at home should be unhurried ones.
We plan around the view through the window and the movement of light and air, and we avoid things that tire the body — stone-paved floors among them. The aim is design that the body itself registers as comfortable.

Design for one household only

Design made around one person’s way of seeing, and no one else’s.
We do not chase fashions, and we do not impose a style. We design a home to be lived in alongside the hours and the things that matter to the people in it.

Design that is loved for a long time

Design that gains character with the years, and can be loved for a long time.
Rather than newly developed natural materials, we use building materials whose safety and reliability are settled and which will still be supplied years from now, so that a repair is always possible. Looked after properly, they take on a character of their own.

Design that gives form to kindness

A place at the entrance where something can be set out for a guest; a living room that lets the season in.
The consideration Japan has long shown a guest, and the person one lives with — it is the sense of beauty that comes from that kindness that we design.
