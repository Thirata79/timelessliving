Why we build this way

Timeless Living — why we build for a home that is comfortable now and in years to come

Many well-built apartments that should last for decades lose their appeal and their value, and are given up on, for no better reason than dated interiors and fittings. Having spent years in apartment construction, we found that hard to watch.

Looking for a home that could be lived in and handed on for a long time, what we arrived at was the strength of natural materials — lime plaster, solid timber flooring, stone — the materials Japan has built with since ancient times.
The natural colour of a real material, its warmth under the hand, the way it takes up and gives back moisture — these reach the senses. Together with a plan that draws in the view, the light, the humidity and the air, they bring a calm into everyday life.

And rather than the ready-made apartment plan in which the furniture can only go in one place, we aim for a layout with room to move: one that lets you feel the four seasons, with an entrance arranged so that a guest can be properly received.
A home is truly rich, we believe, when it is a place to enjoy ordinary days. What we give form to is a sense of beauty born of kindness — the kindness of a people who have valued courtesy and cared for the natural world.

A home in a compact city apartment where you feel calm, and feel cared for.
That is what sets our apartment renovation apart.
