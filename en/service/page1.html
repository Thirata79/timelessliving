What a renovation costs

What a renovation costs varies a great deal with which fittings are included and how the interior is specified, so it cannot be settled by floor area alone. We know many people find that hard to get a grip on.
We therefore set the cost out in two parts: the interior construction cost, covering the flooring, the lime plaster walls and fittings such as the kitchen and the unit bath; and the additional works cost, covering what each client particularly wanted — underfloor heating, wooden shutters, a stone wall at the entrance.
Below are some of the homes we have renovated: what each client cared about, and what it cost.
