The renovation process

Whether the work is repair, or a move prompted by a change in the family, what we do is not simply making rooms. It is bringing new value to the way you see things and the way you live. To make a home that does that, we believe what matters is not only our proposals as professionals but the conversation with you, and the trust built up on both sides through it.
You are welcome to examine what we know, what we have done, and the homes we have actually built, as we work with you towards your own “Timeless Living”.
※ Steps 1 to 4 are free of charge.

Step1※　Enquiry and first conversation

Tell us first what kind of renovation you have in mind and what the ideal home would be. We visit the apartment, photograph it and take measurements. If you have drawings, please have them to hand.

Step2※　Property survey

We investigate what governs the work: the building’s structural conditions, the rules of the building management association. Having checked the state of the structural frame and the services, we judge how far the renovation can go.

Step3※　Planning

We put forward specific proposals — the plan, the fittings, the materials, the interior design.

Step4※　Preliminary quotation

We present a preliminary quotation.

Step 5　Design development

Through a series of meetings we work the plan up in detail.(A design retainer is payable at this stage.)

Step 6　Quotation

On the basis of the detailed plan we present a further quotation.

Step 7　Construction

Once the plan and the budget are settled, we sign a contract and build the work under our own site management.

Step 8　Completion, handover, moving in

When the work is finished you inspect it, and we hand the home over. Aftercare once you have moved in is handled by us directly.
